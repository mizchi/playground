VERDICT: better

The extraction is a real reuse win, not metric gaming: `summarizeByKey` captures the one actual algorithm (accumulate `price*qty` into a bucket keyed by something), and the three exported functions become honest one-line policies over that shared loop. There's no artificial function-splitting to dodge a complexity threshold, no indirection that hides behavior, and the `keyOf(order, item)` callback signature is appropriately general (covers both order-level and item-level keys) without being over-engineered. Bonus: a bug fix to the accumulation logic (e.g. the `?? 0` simplification) now only needs to happen once instead of three times, which was the real risk in the original triplicated code. The one minor cost is a level of indirection — to see what `summarizeByCountry` actually does you now hop into `summarizeByKey` — but that's a normal, worthwhile tradeoff for eliminating copy-pasted loops, not a red flag.
